  1. Connect the Quest to the same LAN as the robot computer. In WebUI, select your Quest profile and click Connect all.
  2. On the headset, open Meta Quest Browser and enter this address:
  3. Select “Enter VR / Start Tracking” on the headset and allow tracking if prompted. Move the controller and look for its trail below.
  4. In the WebUI bottom bar, enable teleoperation. Hold the side grip and move the controller to follow; release the grip to hold position.
